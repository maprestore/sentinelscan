# Code of Conduct

SentinelScan is a defensive security project. We want it to be a place where people can ask questions, disagree about severity ratings, and improve each other's work without fear of harassment or ridicule.

## Our standard

Be the kind of collaborator you would want reviewing your code.

**We encourage**

- Respectful, specific, constructive feedback
- Assuming good faith, especially from newcomers to security
- Explaining the *why* behind a decision, not just the *what*
- Crediting others' work and ideas
- Admitting mistakes and correcting them openly

**We do not tolerate**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention

## Security-specific expectations

Because of what this project does, we also expect the following:

- **Only discuss authorized targets.** Do not post the URL of a system you are not authorized to test, and do not ask for help scanning one.
- **Keep fixtures synthetic.** Never paste real credentials, tokens, cookies, customer data, or private response bodies into issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- **No offensive feature requests.** Requests for exploitation, credential attacks, denial of service, or guardrail bypasses are out of scope and will be closed. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Disclose responsibly.** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for anything that could harm a user if made public.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately, for example through the repository's **Security** tab (*Report a vulnerability*) if you cannot use another private channel. Reports are handled with discretion, and reporters are protected from retaliation.
